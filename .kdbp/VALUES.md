# Project Values

- **V1 — Show the trade-off, not just the winner:** Users must see both sides of every architecture decision — what they gain AND what they sacrifice. `story`
- **V2 — Show what you don't know, not just what you do:** Surface uncertainty, confidence gaps, and data limits rather than presenting partial data as complete. `story`
- **V3 — Show the ripple, not just the piece:** Every change propagates — show downstream effects on connected components, not just the edited node. `story`
- **V4 — Show the roads, not just the dot:** Users need growth paths and actionable next steps, not just current-state snapshots. `epic`
- **V5 — Prove it works, don't trust it reads:** Runtime evidence (E2E, screenshots, live behavior) over code-level assertions. Verify features actually function. `story`
